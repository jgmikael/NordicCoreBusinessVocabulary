## 1. The Challenge
Public procurement represents nearly **14 % of EU GDP**, yet fewer than **5 % of tenders** attract cross-border bids.  
SMEs face persistent obstacles:

- Fragmented tendering portals and document formats  
- Repetition of the same data submissions  
- Cross-border verification of tax, insurance, or compliance status  
- Lack of transparent and comparable qualification criteria  

Result: **high administrative burden**, **low SME participation**, and **lost innovation potential**.

## 2. The Vision: Trusted, Data-Driven eProcurement
By **2030**, European eProcurement will be:

> “A seamless, data-driven ecosystem where every actor exchanges trusted, semantically interoperable credentials via digital agents.”

### Core enabler: EU Business Wallet (EUBW)
A secure wallet for **legal entities** and **authorized representatives**, fully compliant with **eIDAS 2.0** and the **EUDI framework**.  
It stores and presents **Verifiable Credentials (VCs)** that prove facts about a company or a person acting on its behalf.

## 3. All Procurement Data as Verifiable Credentials
Every piece of information in a procurement process can be expressed as a **VC**—digitally signed, machine-verifiable, and reusable across borders.

### 3.1 Foundational Company Credentials
| Category | Example VC | Authentic Source |
|-----------|-------------|------------------|
| Legal Existence | **EU Company Certificate (EUCC)** | National Business Register |
| Representation Rights | **Power of Representation VC** | Mandate Registry / EUDI Wallet |
| Beneficial Ownership | **UBO VC** | Central UBO Register |
| Financial Account | **IBAN VC** | Bank / Payment Institution |
| Tax Identity | **VAT / EORI VC** | Tax Authority |
| Financial Standing | **Balance Sheet VC**, **Turnover VC** | Business Register / Tax Authority |

### 3.2 Procurement-Specific Credentials
| Category | Example VC | Issuer / Source |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| Technical & Professional Ability | **Past Performance VC**, **Reference Contract VC** | Contracting Authority |
| Compliance | **Tax Compliance VC**, **Social Security VC** | Public Authority |
| Sustainability & ESG | **Sustainability VC**, **ESG Attestation VC** | Accredited Body / Auditor |
| Qualifications | **ISO Certificate VC**, **Staff Competence VC** | Standardization Body |
| Tender Submission | **Bid VC**, **Digital Offer VC** | Supplier EUBW |
| Award & Execution | **Contract Award VC**, **Delivery VC**, **Invoice VC**, **Payment VC** | Buyer / Supplier / Bank |

Each VC uses **semantically aligned terms** from:
- **EU Core Vocabularies**  
- **Nordic Core Business Ontology (NCBV)**  
- **UN/CEFACT BSP**, **UBL 2.4**, and **GS1 Web Vocabulary**

## 4. The Agentic AI Layer
Agentic AI introduces **autonomous digital agents** that operate securely on behalf of users.

### Supplier Agent
- Scans EU tender databases (TED, SIMAP)  
- Interprets eligibility criteria  
- Compiles and presents required VCs automatically  
- Alerts human users only when strategic decisions are needed  

### Buyer Agent
- Verifies VCs cryptographically  
- Checks ESG and legal compliance  
- Evaluates offers transparently under human oversight  

### Oversight Agent
- Detects fraud patterns and conflicts of interest  
- Enables real-time audit trails while respecting data minimization  

All agents comply with **AI Act**, **GDPR**, and **Data Governance Act** principles:  
**Explainable, Accountable, Traceable**.

## 5. The Procurement Journey of the Future
1. **Pre-qualification** — Supplier agent submits VCs (EUCC, Tax, UBO, Financial Standing).  
2. **Tender Discovery** — AI matches company profile with new tenders using CPV/eForms metadata.  
3. **Bid Preparation** — “Digital Offer VC” created, signed, and timestamped via EBSI.  
4. **Evaluation & Award** — Buyer agent verifies all VCs, applies criteria, records decision VC.  
5. **Contract Execution & Payment** — Delivery, Invoice, and Payment VCs flow between wallets; smart contracts ensure compliance.

## 8. Governance and Trust
- **Issuers:** National authorities, registries, banks, and QTSPs  
- **Standards:** W3C VC Data Model 2.0 + EU ARF for EUDI  
- **Semantics:** RDF/OWL ontologies (NCBV, EU Core Vocabularies)  
- **Infrastructure:** EBSI / European Blockchain Services Infrastructure  
- **Oversight:** Supervised by EDIC structures and national trust frameworks  

Trust is anchored in **cryptographic verification**, **authoritative sources**, and **transparent semantics**.
